## Why it is allowed here

Showing a client's mark on a case study about work done for that client is
nominative use: it identifies who the work was for. It is not a claim of
endorsement, and it is what every design portfolio does.

**What keeps it honest is the placement.** The mark sits in the same block as:

> Reconstruction by Priyanshu Rai. Not a ZEISS product, and not the interface
> that shipped.

Those two must never be separated. A ZEISS mark alone on the chrome of a
viewer Priyanshu rebuilt would read as a ZEISS product to anyone skimming.

## Rules

- Do not recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects to it. It is shown at its
  own square proportions with no filter.
- Do not put it on anything that is not about the ZEISS work.
- If ZEISS ever object, remove the file. The `<img>` removes itself on error
  and the typographic credit still stands, so nothing breaks.
