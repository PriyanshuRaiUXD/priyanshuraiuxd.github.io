Case file № 01 · June 2026 · sole designer-engineer · milestone 1 of 5

Habit Orchestrator

A linking engine that refuses to lie about your habits.

8 cards · 17 recorded decisions · 3 specifications, 205 KB · 14 source files · 5 canonical tests, all passing

  1. 01 · The problem

    Habit trackers count streaks and stay silent about connections. The moment a system starts inferring connections, it usually starts lying.

    A spurious link presented confidently is not a UX flaw — in this domain it is harm. The red line the whole system is designed against: one-time drinking must never be linked to alcoholism.

  2. 02 · The constraint envelope

    Three constraints preceded any design: the system must be able to refuse to conclude; every link needs a mechanism, not just a correlation; the user owns the record.

    Owning the record means consent before sensitive inference — a Stage-0 pre-pass, upstream of generation, not a checkbox at display time — and true erasure after: aspiration and sensitive-origin claims are hard-deleted cryptographically, because an indelible record of a person's gap between who they are and who they want to be is coercive.

  3. 03 · The pivotal insight

    "How often does B follow A?" and "does the A→B link exist at all?" are different questions. Conflating them was the engine's original sin — it now asks both, separately.

    Existence is decided by model comparison — does a model with this edge explain the data better than one without it? — against a prior that is deliberately stiff for anything shaped like pathology and cheap for benign links. Coffee→focus commits in days. Anything resembling escalation faces a skeptic.

  4. 04 · The pivotal decision

    One gate, three independent tests, all mandatory: significance, effect size, maturity. The weighted-score alternative was rejected because it lets one loud signal buy its way past the other two.

    A candidate edge is a defendant. Is the association beyond chance? Is it strong enough to be a real chain — with separate floors for triggers and reinforcements, because they measure different things? Is there enough data to be sure — confidence shrunk by n/(n+4)? Fail any one test: no commitment. The tiers are words, not scores: committed, hypothesis, abstain.

  5. 05 · The artifact

    The seed data plants traps on purpose: a strong real chain, a significant-but-weak coincidence, a clean-but-young pattern, a single drink.

    The demo runs them all through the gate. It commits the chain, abstains on the coincidence, holds the young pattern, and refuses the drink — each with its reasoning stated in a sentence, because a verdict without a reason is just a score.

  6. 06 · The outcome ledger

    The guarantees hold under test. Reproducible with one command.

    Register honestly: these are design guarantees verified by canonical tests — not shipped-product adoption metrics. M1's job was to prove the reasoning gate before any interface earns the right to exist.

      4. 4 · The raw record beneath

        "The canonical red line: one-time drinking must never link to alcoholism." — and red-team finding #20: an early draft claimed BF≈17 for this case, an impossible number (max is 10). Caught, corrected in place, preserved.
        Spec line 14 + §11 red-team ledger, finding #20 · 2026-06-27
    • Committed

      A 29-of-30 chain commits at confidence ≥ 0.85 — strong, frequent, mature.

    • Abstained

      A statistically significant 16% follow-rate is refused as a chain: "too weak to be a real link." Significance without effect is not a connection.

    • Hypothesis

      A clean 5-of-7-day pattern is held, not committed — maturity shrinkage keeps seven days of data honest, and the verdict states exactly which test it still needs.

  7. 07 · What I got wrong

    My first ledger conflated the two probabilities my own insight said to separate. Case e_0427 exposed it: reported 0.82; the stated update actually gives ≈0.58.

    The 0.82 was the existence posterior wearing the conditional-rate posterior's name. The fix: two fields under true names — p_edge_exists and p_b_given_a — never interchanged, and every worked example regenerated with the arithmetic shown. The erratum is preserved in place, beside a second one, because a spec that hides its errors can't be trusted about anything else.

  8. 08 · The residue

    M1 proves the gate. It does not yet condition on confounders, test causal direction, or notice when what you say contradicts what you log.

    Those are specified, unbuilt, and listed — four milestones remain. A system that cannot say "not yet" shouldn't be trusted to say "no."
